Use case: stylized-concept. A purpose-drawn portrait level cover for an existing Chinese pixel-art RPG, room 001 翠雾渡口 (Jade-mist Ferry).
OUTPUT: one single edge-to-edge PORTRAIT image, width:height 3:5, no text, no UI, no outer door frame, no border, no montage.
Reference 1 is the SUBJECT AUTHORITY: the existing room, a mossy wooden festival ferry with a distinctive large ivory fox-mask figurehead bearing vermilion markings, red braided soul-guiding ropes and tassels, old roof tiles, blank paper lanterns, green river and tree silhouettes. Preserve that place and iconic objects, but create an entirely new vertical cover composition, not a crop of the reference's wide battlefield.
Reference 2 is PIXEL-DRAWING / MATERIAL DISCIPLINE ONLY: broad coherent light/shadow masses, rich functional architecture, controlled edge clusters. Do NOT copy snow, machines, ships from reference 2, its palette or its geography.
Composition: a close three-quarter view of the bow from its landing dock. The large complete ivory fox mask is the clear focal point in the upper-middle; both ears and muzzle fully in frame, comfortably inside an 8% safe margin. Its carved head is mounted to the actual timber prow, not a floating monster. Behind and above it the mossy tiled cabin roof and a few readable lanterns recede diagonally to the upper right. A narrow weathered landing plank with a red-rope mooring post enters from lower right and leads toward the ferry. Dark jade water, broken amber lantern reflections and soft layered green mist form the lower half around the prow. At most 15% empty foreground; no huge flat fighting platform. Make the eye travel from a foreground rope, to the mask, to the lit cabin. Strong silhouette and three depth planes; mysterious, inviting, slightly uncanny, not busy.
Medium: deliberately HAND-PLACED PIXEL ART with visibly stepped contour edges and designed connected square pixel clusters. Think a carefully drawn approx 300 by 500 pixel game illustration presented crisply. Use 3-5 defined shade steps per material, large connected shadow shapes, selective small edge highlights. No smooth digital painting, no watercolor, no anti-aliased brush lines, no photographic texture, no noise or checkerboard overlay, no fake mosaic filter. Preserve convincing timber, roof, rope and water structures without fine grain.
Palette: deep forest and jade green, warm ivory fox mask, restrained cinnabar rope, amber lantern glow; dark forms remain separated and readable at a final 274x460 display. Reserve highest contrast for fox mask and one lantern. Sparse purposeful details, no random sparkles or bright cracks.
No characters, no human faces, no added enemies, no lettering or numbers, no decorative card border.
